Terms and Privacy

Version 1, November 2026

What MyStop Is

MyStop is a free, unofficial student project. It is not run by, endorsed by or affiliated with Unstop or any organiser. Listings, logos and names belong to their owners, and every comp links to its original page.

No Guarantees

Scores, eligibility, dates, prizes and the in-person check are worked out by automatic rules and can be wrong or out of date. Always read the original listing before you register, pay or travel. MyStop is provided as it is, with no warranty, and is not responsible for a missed deadline, a wrong detail or any loss that follows from using it.

What Is Stored

Without signing in, your profile, stars, hidden comps and registrations stay in this browser and are sent nowhere.

If you sign in with Google, the same list is saved in a Google Firebase database under your account, together with your Google name and email address, so it follows you across devices. The numbers of the comps you registered for are also kept in a separate list that holds nothing else, so the refresh can keep those comps up to date.

Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising. There is no tracking and no analytics.

Who Can Sign In

Sign-in is for people aged 18 or older. If you are younger, use MyStop without signing in.

Your Controls

Delete My Data in the account menu erases your account record and your sign-in. The button below erases everything saved in this browser.

Fair Use

Do not try to break, overload or misuse the site or anyone else's data.

Contact and Removal

For questions, corrections or a request to remove a listing, organisers and Unstop included, open an issue at github.com/raunak-comp/mystop. Removal requests are acted on promptly.
